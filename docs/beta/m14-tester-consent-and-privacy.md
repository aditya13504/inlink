## What you are testing

InLink is experimental messaging software. Its features, compatibility, reliability, and security
may change. Do not use it for emergencies, safety-critical activity, or sensitive/high-risk
communications. Do not enter real secrets or information you cannot afford to lose or expose.

## Important limitations

- The cryptographic design and implementation have not been independently audited.
- Relays may observe connection and routing metadata and can delay, drop, or withhold traffic.
- Messages are deliverable only when a viable BLE or internet route exists; recipient delivery must
  be confirmed by an authenticated receipt.
- Mobile operating-system restrictions, device compatibility, and background behavior may interrupt
  communication.
- Test builds may contain bugs and may require reinstalling or clearing test data.
